# Decision 2: savings flow-through for Diageo (Phase 3, 12 Oct 2026)

**The question:** Diageo targets c.$1bn of annual cost savings by FY29. How much of it reaches operating profit, rather than being reinvested (marketing, innovation, price) or eaten by cost inflation? The model adds `savings x flow-through` to a constant 29.4% margin, so flow-through is the *net* margin gain after everything else. The brief has 50% (bear 25%).

All values are pence per ordinary share, using the terminal capex decision (ROIC 13.4%). Price 1,673p; a SELL is -10% or worse.

## Options
| Flow-through | What it matches | FY27 op. profit growth | FY26-29 op. profit CAGR | Value | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50% (today) | Midpoint of FY27 guidance | +3.5% | 4.2% | 1,540p | HOLD (-8.0%) |
| **46% (RECOMMENDED)** | **Low end of management's FY27-29 profit guidance, the same low-end rule you set for sales growth** | **+3.2%** | **4.0%** | **1,526p** | **HOLD (-8.8%)** |
| 33% | Diageo's own 2017 rule: "two thirds being reinvested" | +2.3% | 3.3% | 1,486p | SELL (-11.2%) |
| 25% (brief's bear case) | Track record: savings mostly absorbed in FY22-24 | +1.7% | 2.8% | 1,459p | SELL (-12.8%) |
| 0% | Everything reinvested | 0% | 1.3% | 1,379p | SELL (-17.6%) |

The rating flips from HOLD to SELL at about 40%.

## Evidence
**What management guides (primary, SEC filings):**
1. *Capital Markets Day 6-K, 6 Aug 2026* (SEC accession 0001654954-26-007324): FY27 "broadly flat organic net sales" and "organic operating profit growth up low-to mid-single-digit, including c.40% of the c.$850 million cost savings ... and c.25% of the c.$150 million supply chain savings" (= $377.5m). On flat sales, profit growth of 2% / 3.5% / 5% implies flow-through of 29% / 50% / 72%.
2. Same 6-K, FY27-29 CAGR: "low-single-digit organic net sales growth" and "mid-single-digit organic operating profit growth reflecting the benefit of savings and more favourable mix". On our sales path, a 4% profit CAGR (the low end of mid-single-digit) implies **46%**, and 5% implies 64%. Part of that guidance comes from mix, not savings, so these figures are an upper bound for the savings alone.
3. *20-F FY26, CEO letter:* "we will invest $1.2 billion in restructuring to generate $1 billion in annual savings. We will invest this back to advance innovation, our overall competitiveness and to protect underlying profitability." This argues clearly against 100%.

**Track record (primary unless marked):**
4. *FY17 results 6-K, 27 Jul 2017:* "raising our productivity goal to £700 million with two thirds being reinvested in the business": a planned flow-through of about 33%.
5. *20-F FY23:* "£450 million in productivity savings in fiscal 23 ... fuelled a 6% increase in marketing spend and delivered organic operating margin expansion of 15bps". 15bps on c.$20.6bn of sales is about $30m, so roughly 5% of the savings reached the margin in a high-inflation year.
6. *Operating margin before exceptionals (our ratios, 20-F):* FY22 31.1%, FY23 30.7%, FY24 29.3%, FY25 28.2%, FY26 28.9%. The margin fell even though Diageo reports $1.7bn of productivity benefits over FY22-24 (FY24 annual report, seen through a search summary).

## Recommendation
**46%: the flow-through that makes our model deliver exactly the low end of management's FY27-29 profit guidance.** It applies the rule you already set for sales growth (low end of guidance) to profit as well, so the two assumptions are consistent. It also sits inside the FY27 guidance range (+3.2% vs "low-to-mid single digit").

It is below the 50% midpoint because the track record (items 4-6) shows Diageo has historically reinvested most of its savings. It is above the 25-33% history because this programme is explicitly a turnaround with a profit commitment, and management has guided profit growing faster than sales for three years.

**Main risk:** if Diageo repeats FY23 (savings absorbed by inflation and marketing), flow-through falls towards 25%, and the call is a SELL at 1,459p. Combined with the terminal capex risk, the HOLD rests on management delivering at least the low end of its own guidance.
