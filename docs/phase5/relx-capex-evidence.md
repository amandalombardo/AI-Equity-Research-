## The consistency rule (from Unilever, mistakes log row 23)
Whatever is in the depreciation and amortisation (D&A) we add back must have its spending in capex. RELX's adjusted operating profit (the 35.0% margin) includes:
- amortisation of capitalised development (software and products), £352m, matched by capitalised development spend of £504m;
- PP&E depreciation £26m, matched by PP&E capex £21m;
- **pre-publication cost amortisation £89m**: its spending (£102m) is **not** in RELX's capex line, so we add it;
- **lease (right-of-use) depreciation £37m**: lease liabilities are already in net debt, so we leave this out of the D&A add-back and treat it as a running cost.

Amortisation of acquired intangibles (£248m) is not in adjusted profit, so it is not added back either.

## Acquisitions
RELX spends steadily on small acquisitions: £254m, £394m, £124m, £170m, £260m in FY21-25 (SEC XBRL, cash paid for businesses), on average **2.7% of revenue** (computed). Disposal proceeds after 2020 are not tagged; only FY24's £95m is known (MISSING for other years).

Do our numbers need acquisitions? The agreed 5.0% growth is constant-currency growth. In FY25 that was 4% = 7% organic ("underlying") minus 3 points from disposals, exhibition timing and print, with acquired revenue adding about nothing (FY25 results p.2-3). So the 5.0% is reached organically, without buying revenue.
