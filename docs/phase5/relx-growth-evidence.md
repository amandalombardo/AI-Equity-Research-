# RELX: revenue growth evidence (14 Oct 2026)

All figures in GBP from RELX's SEC filings (detail and page references in relx-facts.md). "Computed" = desk arithmetic.

## What RELX's revenue has done
| Year | Underlying | Constant currency | Currency effect | Reported in GBP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY21 | +7% | +8% | -6 pts | +2% |
| FY22 | +9% | +11% | +7 pts | +18% |
| FY23 | +8% | +7% | about 0 | +7% |
| FY24 | +7% | +6% | -3 pts | +3% |
| FY25 | +7% | +4% | -2 pts | +2% |
| H1 2026 | +7% | +5% | -2 pts | +3% |

- **Underlying** excludes acquisitions, disposals, exhibition cycling and (from 2025) print. It is the figure RELX headlines, but the DCF needs the growth of the revenue it is actually valuing.
- **Constant currency** includes print run-off and disposals, so it is the right "business" growth for the DCF. Average FY24 to H1 2026: **5.0%** (computed). FY21-25 average 7.2% (computed), lifted by the post-Covid exhibitions recovery in FY21-23.
- **Print** is being run off: £517m (FY24), £399m (FY25, -23%), H1 2026 £157m vs £189m (-17%). It is now about 3% of revenue, so its drag on group growth shrinks each year.
- **Currency:** about 58% of revenue is North American. The currency effect averaged -0.8 pts a year over FY21-25 (computed) but swung from -6 to +7: no steady trend, unlike Unilever's emerging-market currencies (-2.4 pts every year, which we deducted).
- **Outlook (H1 2026, reaffirmed):** "another year of strong underlying growth in revenue". No numeric guidance.
- **AI risk:** the share price fell 14% on 3 Feb 2026 on AI fears (press reports), but Legal growth sped up to +9% (FY25) and +10% (H1 2026).
