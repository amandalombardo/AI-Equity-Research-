## 8. Things that stand out (facts, not conclusions)

1. **The share price has fallen by about a third since the end of 2025 because of AI fears, while results kept improving.** REL.L closed at 3,020p on 31 Dec 2025 and 2,585p on 2 Feb 2026, then **2,214p on 3 Feb 2026 (-14% in a day)** (Yahoo). Press reports link the fall to Anthropic launching an AI legal plugin for its Cowork tool. Peers Wolters Kluwer, Thomson Reuters, LSEG and Experian also fell ([Hargreaves Lansdown news](https://www.hl.co.uk/shares/stock-market-news/company--news/archive/relx,-pearson-and-informa-slide-as-anthropic-launches-ai-legal-tool); [ShareScope 18 Feb 2026](https://knowledge.sharescope.co.uk/2026/02/18/bi-weekly-market-commentary-18-02-2026-rel-sdr-rsw-saas-pocalypse/); secondary sources). The lowest close between 28 Jan and 13 Feb 2026 was 2,013p on 11 Feb (Yahoo; the full-year low was not checked). RELX's own stated market capitalisation was about £64bn in Oct 2025 (TU-Oct25) and about £49bn in Apr 2026 (TU-Apr26). Average buyback price fell from 3,797p (2025) to 2,529p (H1 2026).
2. **Management's position on AI** (FY25R p.1 CEO quote): AI *"has been a key driver of our business for well over a decade, and will remain a key driver of customer value and growth in our business for many years to come."* Legal growth sped up to +9% (FY25) and +10% (H1 2026), driven by "Lexis+ with Protégé" (H126 p.7). The 2026 principal-risk wording on customer demand added "advances in artificial intelligence, workflow tools" and the need to "effectively integrate evolving technologies" (H126 p.15). IP risk mentions being "circumvented, including by companies leveraging technology tools and AI" (FY25R p.16). Large language model providers are now named as a supply-chain dependency (FY25R p.17).
3. **Currency: about 58% of revenue is North American** *(computed from FY25R p.26)*, so a stronger pound cuts sterling results. Currency took 6 points off revenue growth in FY21, added 7 in FY22, took 3 off in FY24, 2 in FY25 and 2 in H1 2026. Average rate US$1.28/£ (FY24), $1.32 (FY25), $1.35 (H1 2026) (FY25R p.30; H126 p.29). Sensitivity: a 10% move in all currencies against sterling changes net profit by about £170m (AR25 p.172). Most debt is in USD and EUR, which gives a partial balance-sheet hedge (FY25R p.12). In 2025 RELX swapped US$500m into EUR and US$500m into JPY. These swaps do not get hedge accounting, so their value changes flow through reported interest (H1 2026 gain £31m) (FY25R p.12; H126 p.9).
4. **Leverage is rising because of buybacks**: net debt/EBITDA 1.8x (FY24) → 2.0x (FY25) → 2.3x (Jun 2026). 2026 buybacks of £2,250m are about 97% of FY25 free cash flow before dividends (£2,313m) *(computed)*, so most of the buyback is debt-funded once dividends of about £1.2bn are paid.
5. **Deals are small**: acquisitions £270m in 2025 (five deals), £103m in H1 2026 (two deals); 2024 had seven disposals for £95m (FY25R p.2; H126 p.2; FY24R). No large acquisitions or disposals since at least 2021 (FY22 was the largest year at £443m across nine deals, FY22R).
