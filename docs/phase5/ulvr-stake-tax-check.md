# Unilever evidence check: TMICC stake (brief item E) and operating tax rate (item F), 13 October 2026

## E. The 19.85% stake in The Magnum Ice Cream Company (TMICC)
| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Stake kept at the demerger (6 Dec 2025) | **19.85%** (80.15% given to Unilever shareholders) | H1 2026 6-K (0000217410-26-000057), note 6 |
| Shares held | **121,604,413** TMICC shares (19.86% of shares outstanding at 30 Mar 2026) | TMICC resale prospectus, Form 424(b)(4), 13 Apr 2026 (registered for resale by Unilever; TMICC receives nothing) |
| Book value (fair value) | €1,655m (31 Dec 2025); **€1,852m** (30 Jun 2026) | H1 2026 6-K, financial instruments note |
| Share price | **€15.914** (13 Oct 2026, Yahoo MICC.AS) | |
| Value at market | €15.914 x 121.6m = **c.€1,935m (EUR)**, c.76p a Unilever share | Desk calculation; the valuation agent recomputes on the run date |

**Cross-check:** the 30 Jun 2026 book value implies €1,852m / 121.6m = €15.23 a share (EUR), close to the market price at that date, so the share count and the stake agree with Unilever's own accounts.

**Sell-down risk:** Unilever says the stake "will be sold down in an orderly and considered manner to pay demerger costs" (FY25 results 6-K). We found no sale up to 13 Oct 2026: the H1 2026 report still shows the stake, and searches of news and TMICC's September 2026 6-Ks found no placing. A sale at market would swap shares for cash of the same value, so it would not change the valuation. The Data agent re-checks for any sale on the run date.

**Why market value:** it is a listed stake with a daily price, the same rule used for KO's listed stakes. Book value (€1,852m at 30 Jun) is itself a fair-value mark, so the two methods differ by only about €80m.

## F. Operating tax rate: the evidence points to 26.0%, not 25.7%
| | FY21 | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Underlying effective tax rate | 22.6% | 24.1% | 25.6% | 25.8% (25.9% restated ex Ice Cream) | **25.7%** | **26.0%** |

Sources: annual results 6-Ks FY21-FY25 (accessions in ulvr-growth-evidence.md), H1 2026 6-K.

**Management guidance:** "around 25%" (FY22 and FY23 releases), raised to "around 26%" for 2025 (FY24 release, 0001654954-25-001439), and "remains around 26%" for 2026 (H1 2026 6-K). H1 2026 says the rate rose "due primarily to lower benefits from tax settlements": FY25's 25.7% was helped by one-off settlements, which do not recur.

**Correction:** the earlier proposal of 25.7% (FY25 actual) ignored the H1 2026 actual and the guidance. A DCF needs the forward rate, and both the latest actual and management's guide are 26.0%. The rate has risen every year since FY21 except FY25, and the Indian and Indonesian rate cuts behind the FY20-21 lows are already in the base.

| Option | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | **26.0%** | H1 2026 actual and management's 2025 and 2026 guidance; excludes one-off settlement benefits |
| B | 25.7% | FY25 audited actual (the base year), as used for Diageo and KO; includes settlement benefits |

The difference is 0.3 points of tax, so after-tax operating profit is about 0.4% lower under A.
