# Unilever growth evidence (for brief assumption B), 13 October 2026

**Question:** what euro revenue growth should the DCF use? Unilever's headline "underlying sales growth" (USG) excludes currency and acquisitions/disposals, but the DCF is in euros, so currency losses are real.

FY18 A&D is implied from the other three columns (the release gives only "the disposal of spreads"). Components compound: (1 + USG) x (1 + A&D) x (1 + currency) - 1 = turnover change. USG excludes "price growth in excess of 26% in hyperinflationary economies" (definition in the FY23 and FY25 releases); that excess sits in the currency column (for example Q4 2025, Group: exchange rates -7.9 points, extreme hyperinflation pricing +0.5 points, currency total -7.5; FY25 6-K business-group table), so the currency column already nets off part of that inflation.

## Averages (compound, per year)
| Period | USG | Currency | USG + currency (euro growth before deals) | Reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY17-25 (9 years) | 4.3% | **-2.4%** | **1.8%** | 1.2% |
| FY20-25 (6 years, like KO's window) | 5.0% | -2.4% | 2.5% | 2.0% |
| FY17-25 excluding FY22 (the inflation spike) | 3.7% | -3.5% | 0.2% | -0.4% |

**Finding:** currency has cost Unilever about 2.4 points a year in euros, more than twice KO's 1 point (ko-growth-evidence.md). Over nine years, euro sales before deals grew only 1.8% a year although USG averaged 4.3%.

## Guidance
- Multi-year USG range 4% to 6%; FY26 within the range with c.3% volume; H2 2026 4-5% (H1 2026 6-K, outlook). In February the guide was "bottom end" of the range (FY25 6-K).
- The HPC business that remains grows faster than the Foods being sold: the sale perimeter's FY25 USG was 2.7% (Form 425) against 3.5% for the Group, and H1 2026 USG was 5.9% Beauty & Wellbeing, 4.8% Personal Care, 7.6% Home Care, 1.2% Foods.
- No currency guidance found.

## Proposal
| Option | FY26 | FY27-30 | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | **+0.5%** | **1.6%** a year | FY26: the only actual so far is H1 reported turnover +0.5% (USG 4.8% less 4.9% currency). FY27-30: low end of the 4-6% USG range (same low-end rule as Diageo and KO) less the 9-year currency drag of 2.4 points |
| B | +0.5% | 2.5% | Keeps the 6-year USG + currency record (FY20-25), which includes the FY22 inflation year |
| C | +0.5% | 3.6% | Ignores currency beyond 2026 (USG 4% less a 0.4-point drag); hard to justify on the record |

Bear case: 0.2% (the 9-year record excluding FY22).

**Challenge for the PM:** under option A the DCF's euro growth (1.6%) is below the 2.0% terminal growth used for Diageo and KO. Either terminal growth should also come down for Unilever, or we accept that terminal growth reflects long-run inflation once currency drag fades. This goes with decision J (terminal growth), later.
