# RELX: cost of debt evidence (14 Oct 2026)

Method agreed for Unilever: pre-tax cost of debt = risk-free (gilt 5.45%, agreed) + the market credit spread for the company's rating. RELX's own historic borrowing cost is not used, because old fixed coupons lag today's rates (the Diageo lesson, mistakes log).
