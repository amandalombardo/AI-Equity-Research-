# RELX: operating tax rate evidence (14 Oct 2026)

From relx-facts.md section 2 (RELX results releases and Annual Report 2025, page references there).

| | FY21 | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjusted tax rate (RELX) | 18.5%* | 21.3% | 20.4%* | 22.5% | 22.5% | **22.8%** |
| Reported tax rate | 18.1% | 22.8% | 22.1% | 24.0% | 24.4% | 22.8% |

\* lifted by one-off credits from settling old tax matters (RELX's own wording).

- **No numeric guidance.** But the H1 2026 tax charge is, in RELX's words, its "best estimate of the annual tax rate expected for the full financial year" (H1 2026 note 7): **22.8%**. That is the closest thing to a forward rate.
- Weighted statutory rate across RELX's countries: 25.0% (FY25), 24.7% (H1 2026). The global minimum tax (Pillar Two) has "no significant impact" (AR25 p.159).
- Cash tax paid: £638m in FY25, about 23% of reported profit before tax (computed: 638 / 2,750).
- The adjusted rate matches the adjusted (amortisation-free) operating profit the DCF uses, so it is the consistent choice.
