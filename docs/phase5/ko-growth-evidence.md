# Coca-Cola: evidence for the revenue growth path (USD)

Prepared 13 Oct 2026 for Amanda's decision 5A in brief-coca-cola.md.

## The problem
Coca-Cola's headline "organic" growth strips out currency. In countries with high inflation (Argentina above all) prices rise fast, but the local currency falls about as fast, so in US dollars much of that growth disappears. The DCF is in US dollars, so it needs growth in dollars, not organic growth.

What it shows:
- **Currency took 3.2 points a year off growth on average (2020-25).** In the Argentina years (2022-24) it was 4 to 7 points. In the other years (2020, 2021, 2025) it averaged 1 point.
- **Dollar growth before disposals compounded at c.5.1% a year over 2020-25.** That period includes the 2021-23 inflation surge and the bounce back from Covid, so it flatters the steady state.
- Q4 2024: "more than half" of the 23% price/mix growth came from "inflationary pricing in Argentina" (Q4 2024 release).

## What management guides
- FY26 (raised 28 Jul 2026, accession 0001628280-26-049922): organic c.5%, currency +1%, disposals -2% to -3%. So reported growth of c.+3.5% (mid-points).
- FY27 onwards: no published guidance. Management called its original FY26 range of 4-5% "in line with our long-term growth algorithm" (Q4 2025 call, reported by press; not yet found in a primary source).

## Options for FY27-FY30 (USD growth, ex disposals)

| Option | Growth a year | How it is built | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4.0% | Low end of the 4-5% range, no currency drag | Assumes currencies stop falling. History says they don't |
| **B (recommended)** | **3.0%** | Low end of the range (4%) minus the 1-point currency drag seen in non-hyperinflation years | Same "low end of guidance" rule you used for Diageo. Below the 5.1% achieved in 2020-25, which was flattered by inflation |
| C | 1.0% | 4% minus the full 3.2-point 2020-25 average drag | Too punitive: it removes the Argentina currency fall but not the Argentina price rises that came with it |

FY26: +3.5% per guidance under every option. Terminal growth: 2.0% as for Diageo. Bear case to test: 1.0% (option C).
