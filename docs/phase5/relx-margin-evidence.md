# RELX: operating margin evidence (14 Oct 2026)

All GBP, from RELX filings (relx-facts.md has page references). "Computed" = desk arithmetic.

## Track record (adjusted operating margin, group)
| FY21 | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | H1 2025 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30.5% | 31.4% | 33.1% | 33.9% | 34.8% | 34.8% | 35.5% |

- The margin has risen every year. H1 2025 equalled the FY25 full-year margin (34.8%), so the first half is not seasonally high: H1 2026 is a fair read of the current year.
- **Outlook (H1 2026):** in Risk, STM and Legal "underlying adjusted operating profit growth exceeding underlying revenue growth"; Exhibitions "an improvement in adjusted operating margin". So management expects the margin to keep rising, but gives no number.
- **Print is a high-margin business being run off:** FY25 £399m revenue, £185m profit (46%); H1 2026 £157m and £78m (50%). It shrinks about 20% a year, so it will barely exist by the end of the forecast. A margin held forever should exclude it.
- **Adjusted profit excludes** amortisation of acquired intangibles (£248m, a non-cash accounting charge from past acquisitions) and acquisition and disposal costs (£54m, 0.6% of revenue). These costs go with buying businesses; they are dealt with in the acquisitions/capex card, not here.

## Margin without print (computed)

| H1 2026 | 35.5% | **35.0%** ((1,727 - 78) / (4,871 - 157)) |

## Options (held flat, the desk's rule for Diageo, Coca-Cola and Unilever)
| Option | Margin | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | **35.0%** | Latest actual (H1 2026) without print. Flat is cautious: management guides higher, and the record is +0.9 points a year |
| B | 34.3% | FY25 full year without print (older evidence) |
| C | 35.5% | H1 2026 group margin, including print that is running off |
| Bear | 33.1% | FY23 level: AI tools force price cuts in Legal and STM (stress test) |

The engine holds the margin flat; modelling further rises would need a code change, and the only basis would be a trend, not a source.
