## Q2. Joint ventures inside adjusted and reported operating profit

**Yes to both.** The equity-accounted JV/associate share is inside **reported** operating profit (post-tax, as an income-statement line above "Operating profit"). **Adjusted** operating profit then grosses it up to a pre-tax, pre-interest basis. **JV share of adjusted operating profit FY25 = £57m (FY24 £54m)**, stated by RELX (AR25 p.206).

- **Reported OP includes the JV share.** FY25R p.19, consolidated income statement:
  > "Share of results of joint ventures and associates | 43 [2024] | 44 [2025]" … "Operating profit | 2,861 | 3,027"
  AR25 p.146 (note 2):
  > "The share of post-tax results of joint ventures and associates included in operating profit was £44m (2024: £43m; 2023: £46m). This comprised of profit/(loss) relating to Risk of £(1)m … Scientific, Technical & Medical £1m … Legal £6m … and Exhibitions £38m"
- **Adjusted OP definition** (AR25 p.199, Alternative performance measures table):
  > "Adjusted operating profit — Operating profit before amortisation of acquired intangible assets, acquisition and disposal related items, and grossed up to exclude the equity share of finance income, finance costs and taxes in joint ventures and associates"
  FY25R p.10:
  > "Adjusted operating profit from joint ventures and associates is grossed up for our equity share of interest and taxation."
- **Reconciliation FY25** (FY25R p.26; repeated FY25R p.31 and AR25 p.199):
  > "Operating profit 2,861 | 3,027; Adjustments: Amortisation of acquired intangible assets 258 | 248; Acquisition and disposal related items 69 | 54; Reclassification of tax in joint ventures and associates 12 | 14; Reclassification of finance income in joint ventures and associates (1) | (1); Adjusted operating profit 3,199 | 3,342"
  JV amortisation of acquired intangibles is also added back inside the £248m (FY25R p.10: "The amortisation charge in respect of acquired intangible assets, including the share of amortisation in joint ventures and associates, was £248m"). AR25 p.149 shows the JV part of that is nil in 2024 and 2025 (£1m in 2023).
- **JV share of adjusted OP, as stated by RELX**: AR25 p.206 (APM, leverage ratio):
  > "EBITDA 3,724 [2024] 3,846 [2025] … Less joint venture and associates adjusted operating profit (54) (57) … EBITDA for leverage ratio 3,601 3,735"
  and: "For the purpose of calculating leverage ratios, share of results in joint ventures and associates, the equity share of finance income, finance costs, taxes and amortisation in joint ventures and associates, and acquisition and disposal related items are deducted from EBITDA" (AR25 p.205).
  Cross-check: £44m post-tax + £14m tax - £1m finance income = £57m (*computed*; matches RELX's figure).
- **H1 2026** (H126 p.24 reconciliation): "Operating profit 1,490 | 1,585 … Reclassification of tax in joint ventures and associates 7 | 8; Reclassification of net finance (income)/costs in joint ventures and associates - | 1; Adjusted operating profit 1,652 | 1,727". Post-tax JV share H1 2026 £26m (H1 2025 £27m) (H126 p.26, note 5: "Share of results of joint ventures and associates (27) | (26)"). RELX does not state an H1 2026 JV adjusted-OP figure: NOT FOUND. (£26m + £8m + £1m = £35m would be *computed*, not RELX-stated.)
- **Valuation implication (for the desk, not a RELX statement):** adjusted OP £3,342m includes about £57m (1.7%) from equity-accounted JVs whose debt and cash are **not** in net debt. Either value the JVs separately and strip them out of EBIT, or accept a small double-count.
