| Challenge | Proposed response | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foods consideration worth less | Accepted as a risk, not a base-case change. The base prices McCormick's shares at the 13 Oct close (decision "today's deal value") and leaves the undisclosed tax out (decision 5), showing 39p per EUR 1bn instead. Tax stress (-138p) and McCormick -1 s.d. (-201p) both stay SELL; a failed deal is roughly neutral. | brief decisions 1 and 5; ulvr-tax-evidence.md |
| 2. 17.7% margin is a peak | Keep 17.7%. Even at 16.2% the rating stays SELL. | ulvr-margin-evidence.md; brief item C |
| 3. HUL minorities at market | Keep proportionate minorities, the desk rule set for Diageo (decision 4). HUL's market value reflects India's growth premium, which a proportionate share of Unilever's value does not; market value is shown as the bear line and stays SELL. | ulvr-minorities-evidence.md |
| 4. RONIC = WACC is an extreme case: the bear's own low bound for ROIC without Foods goodwill is 16.5% (-20p). Both stay SELL. | brief decision 6; bear/notes.md item 5 |
| 5. Discount rate | Keep beta 0.8844 (latest Damodaran global Household Products, Jan 2026, decision 8). The bear's 0.9998 uses the 2020-24 average, which is older evidence; the larger risk is the other way (Unilever's own weekly betas 0.43-0.54, R-squared 0.08-0.30, too noisy to use). SELL either way. | risk/notes.md; betaGlobal.xls |
