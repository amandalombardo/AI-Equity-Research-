## What I checked and found correct

- **WACC and CAPM:** CAPM is risk-free rate plus beta times ERP. The code applies the debt tax shield and the supplied debt/equity weights correctly. On the run inputs, cost of equity is 10.0205%, after-tax debt cost is 4.7085%, and WACC is 8.4590%.
- **Forecast cash flow:** NOPAT + D&A − capex − change in NWC uses current-year sales for D&A and capex, and current-year less prior-year sales for the NWC change. For FY27, growth is zero, so NWC investment is zero. FY27 savings kept are USD 377.5m × 50% = USD 188.75m; FY29 onward is USD 1,000m × 50% = USD 500m, added to the constant base-margin EBIT as specified.
- **Stub and discount timing:** With 0.2822 years elapsed, the first year’s cash flow is counted at 71.78% and discounted at 0.7178 years under the end-year convention. The terminal value is discounted to the end of FY31 at 4.7178 years. Mid-year-period formulas are covered by a passing test.
- **Equity bridge and units:** Leases are not subtracted twice because the sourced net debt includes them; zero additional lease liabilities and zero pension deficit follow the provided inputs. The bridge subtracts net debt and minorities and adds associates and disposal proceeds. USD per share is converted using USD per GBP and then multiplied by 100 for pence. No ADR multiplier is applied to ordinary-share value.
- **Rating and sensitivity:** The rule compares upside with +15% and downside with −10%, including the boundaries. The centre of the sensitivity grid matches the base case.
- **Run rules:** The credit-spread rule replaces historic debt cost with risk-free rate plus the 1.00% spread. Missing or unsourced inputs stop valuation. The bear case uses zero growth, 25% savings flow-through and a 1.00% WACC increase; it is lower than the base case.
- **Comps and ratios:** The calendarisation helper correctly weights overlapping fiscal years in the supplied June, December, March and April examples. Median, low and high multiples use positive available observations. The fundamentals helpers use the documented numerator and denominator, including average invested capital for ROIC; the supplied ratio output uses same-year amounts for margins, cash conversion, leverage and interest cover.
- **Data-source and Excel checks:** The XBRL selection code distinguishes net sales from revenue including excise, filters by units and dimensions, and subtracts treasury and employee-trust shares per the supplied method. The workbook formulas reproduce the Python formulas, including the terminal-value issue in DCF-01. The Excel parity test passed.
