## Thesis
- **The price needs a much lower cost of capital.** The WACC (the yearly return lenders and shareholders require) is 8.00% in our model; the share price implies about 5.5%. No reasonable single change takes KO out of SELL.
- **The cash yield is below the risk-free rate.** FY26 free cash flow guidance of $12.4bn is a 3.20% yield on the market value, against 5.22% on the 10-year US Treasury, at about 30x earnings, before any IRS payment.
- **A high-quality business, fully priced.** ROIC is 27.0% and the margin ex-CCBA 33.2%, but stacking the four most generous assumptions still gives $79.45, below the HOLD line.

## Fundamentals
- **Growth slowing:** organic 16% (FY21, FY22), 12% (FY23, FY24), 5% (FY25); reported 1.9% in FY25 after currency. Volume flat.
- **Margins up, partly mix:** comparable 28.7% (FY22) to 31.2% (FY25), helped by selling bottlers; impairments of USD 1,045m in FY25 are excluded.
- **Returns high:** ROIC 25.0%, 27.5%, 27.9%, 27.0% (FY22-25); 31.4% in FY25 without the IRS deposit.
- **Cash hit by one-offs, debt steady:** FY25 free cash flow USD 5,296m (USD 11,365m before the fairlife payment); net debt / comparable EBITDA fell from 2.40x (FY21) to 1.85x (FY25).

## Risks
- **Beta** (sensitivity to the market): 0.6091, the US soft-drinks industry beta (0.5755) relevered. KO's own regressions give 0.13 to 0.35, R-squared 1-12%. HOLD needs 0.061.
- **Volatility:** 18.9% (1 year) and 16.7% (5 years) vs 17.0% for the S&P 500 (5 years).
- **Drawdown:** maximum -17.3% over 5 years, $58.08 (22 Apr 2022) to $48.05 (5 Oct 2023), dividend-adjusted.
- **Leverage:** 1.85x net debt / comparable EBITDA (2.00x reported, 2.12x with leases); interest cover 8.3x; rated A+. Paying the USD 14,000m IRS liability would lift it to 2.72x. KO's own target is MISSING.
