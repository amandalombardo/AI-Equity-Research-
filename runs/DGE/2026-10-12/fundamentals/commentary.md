# Fundamentals commentary: Diageo FY22-FY26 (USD m)

**Growth has stalled.** Reported net sales went from 20,516 (FY22) to 19,643 (FY26). Organic growth (as published) went from 6.5% in FY23 to -0.6% (FY24), 1.7% (FY25) and -2.0% (FY26). North America, the largest region at 7,249, fell 8.4% organically in FY26. Asia Pacific fell 6.3%, due to Chinese white spirits. Growth came from Africa (13.3%), Latin America and Caribbean (7.7%) and Europe (3.4%).

**Margins have compressed, and savings have only partly offset this.** Operating margin before exceptionals went from 31.1% (FY22) to 28.2% (FY25), then recovered to 28.9% in FY26 as cost savings came through (organic operating profit grew 2.0%). The reported margin of 16.1% in FY26 is much lower because of exceptional restructuring and impairment charges (2,527).

**Cash generation has improved.** Free cash flow went from 2,235 (FY23) to 3,211 (FY26), helped by lower capex (1,197 vs 1,612 in FY25). Cash conversion of 1.85x in FY26 is flattered: reported net profit (1,737) is depressed by non-cash impairments.

**The balance sheet is stretched.** Adjusted net borrowings / adjusted EBITDA rose from 2.73x (FY23) to 3.35x (FY25), above the 2.5-3.0x target, and eased to 3.14x in FY26. Interest cover fell from 13.6x (FY22) to 7.0x (FY26). This is why the dividend was cut. ROIC (published) is 13.4%.

## Flags
1. FY26 cash conversion (1.85x) reflects exceptional non-cash charges in net profit. It is not a sustainable level.
2. FY23 free cash flow (2,235) was hit by an inventory build (inventories 8,584 to 9,653). Maturing whisky stock ties up cash, which matters for the NWC assumption (37.6% of sales).
3. Reported and organic growth differ by up to 1 point because of disposals (Nigeria, Ghana, Seychelles) and FX. Do not compare them directly.
4. FY22 organic growth and the FY22 leverage ratio were not retrieved (not needed for the valuation).
