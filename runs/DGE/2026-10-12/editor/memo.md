## Thesis
- **Fair value is a little above the price, and that rests on the discount rate.** The DCF gives 1,808.8p (USD 23.94), 8.1% above 1,673p. The price implies a WACC of 8.30% (ours 7.92%), or an ERP of about 4.78% (ours 4.2%). At Kroll's 5.0% ERP the value is 1,625.5p, below the price.
- **Self-help is the whole profit story.** Organic net sales fell 2.0% in FY26 and North America fell 8.4%. The c.USD 1,000m savings plan drives profit growth; we assume 46% of it reaches profit, the low end of FY27-29 guidance.
- **The balance sheet limits the upside.** Leverage is 3.14x against a 2.5-3.0x target and the dividend has been cut. Disposal proceeds (USD 3,318m gross) had not been received at 30 June 2026.

## Fundamentals
- **Growth has stalled:** organic growth was 6.5% (FY23), -0.6%, 1.7% and -2.0% (FY26). Africa (13.3%) and Latin America & Caribbean (7.7%) grew.
- **Margins compressed:** operating margin before exceptionals fell from 31.1% (FY22) to 28.9% (FY26).
- **Cash improved:** free cash flow rose from USD 2,235m (FY23) to USD 3,211m (FY26), helped by lower capex (6.1% of sales in FY26).
- **Returns fell:** ROIC went from 18.4% (FY23) to 13.4% (FY26); interest cover fell from 13.6x (FY22) to 7.0x (FY26).

## Risks
- **Beta:** we use 0.96 (bottom-up), above all six regressions of Diageo's own shares (0.37 to 0.77). 0.72 gives 2,107p (BUY); BUY if beta is below 0.860, SELL above 1.282.
- **Volatility:** 31.6% (1 year) vs 12.5% for the FTSE 100.
- **Drawdown:** maximum -66.8%, from 4,098p (4 Jan 2022) to 1,362p (24 Mar 2026).
- **Leverage:** 3.14x adjusted net borrowings / adjusted EBITDA vs a 2.5-3.0x target. "About 2.6x after the disposals" is a floor: it is before tax on the proceeds and still counts EABL's EBITDA, which leaves with the sale, so true leverage is higher.

## Bear case and our response
No single business challenge now flips the rating to SELL (three did on 13 Oct). All values below are pence (GBp); responses are the PM's of 13 Oct, written against the old values.

1. **Flow-through: most savings are reinvested.** 33% gives 1,762.2p, 25% gives 1,733.6p (HOLD). **Response:** 46% is the low end of FY27-29 profit guidance; we will check it against FY27 interim margins.
2. **Terminal returns: ROIC fell 5 points in three years.** RONIC 10.8% gives 1,735.9p; RONIC equal to WACC gives 1,599.0p (HOLD; was SELL). **Response:** 13.4% is Diageo's lowest ROIC in four years, not a recovery, and FY26 capex (6.1% of sales) is close to the 6.21% the rule implies.
3. **No recovery: both 2023-26 incentive targets missed.** Zero organic growth gives 1,656.8p (HOLD; was SELL). **Response:** the path is already the low end of guidance; five years of zero growth is our bear scenario, not the base.
4. **Terminal growth 2% vs falling spirits volumes.** 1.5% gives 1,735.9p (HOLD). **Response:** with 2.35% breakeven inflation, 2% nominal already means a small real decline; the IWSR figures are secondhand.
5. **Cost of capital.** Kroll's 5.0% ERP gives 1,625.5p (1,545.7p with Kroll's 20-year 5.60%); 5.5% gives 1,525.7p; beta 1.00 gives 1,766.0p; a 2.0% credit spread 1,728.3p (all HOLD). **Response:** our 1% spread is about the BBB average (1.02%, vs 0.69% for A-rated). ERP: PM to respond once 4.2% is confirmed at source.
6. **USL minority at market** (USD 5,778m with others at book): 1,666.8p (HOLD; was SELL). **Response:** an upper bound: USL's minority at market but Diageo's 57.21% at the DCF multiple, and the USD 1,328m still includes EABL's 35%.
7. **Data inputs (audit).** Proceeds are gross (-3.5p per USD 100m of tax); diluted shares (2,231m) give 1,803.1p. **Response:** PM to respond.

**What flips the rating.** Alone, only the discount rate: SELL if ERP is above 5.61% or beta above 1.282; BUY if ERP is below 3.76% or beta below 0.860. SELL otherwise needs a stack: the audit's bear lines with ERP 5.5% (1,439.4p) or with flow-through 33% and beta 1.00 (1,449.8p). A broad business bear at ERP 4.2% gives 1,609.6p, still HOLD but 3.8% below the price.
