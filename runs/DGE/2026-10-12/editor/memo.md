## Thesis
- **The price already assumes the recovery is delivered.** At a WACC of 8.46% the DCF gives 1,524p (USD 20.17), 8.9% below the 1,673p share price. The market is pricing a WACC of about 8.0%.
- **Self-help is the whole profit story.** Organic net sales fell 2.0% in FY26 and North America fell 8.4%. The c.USD 1,000m savings plan drives profit growth, and our 46% flow-through is the low end of management's FY27-29 profit guidance, with no cushion.
- **The balance sheet limits the upside.** Leverage is 3.14x against a 2.5-3.0x target and the dividend has been cut. Disposal proceeds (USD 3,318m gross) had not been received at 30 June 2026.

## Fundamentals
- **Growth has stalled:** organic growth was 6.5% (FY23), -0.6%, 1.7% and -2.0% (FY26). Africa (13.3%) and Latin America & Caribbean (7.7%) grew.
- **Margins compressed:** operating margin before exceptionals fell from 31.1% (FY22) to 28.9% (FY26).
- **Cash improved:** free cash flow rose from USD 2,235m (FY23) to USD 3,211m (FY26), helped by lower capex.
- **Returns fell:** ROIC went from 18.4% (FY23) to 13.4% (FY26); interest cover fell from 13.6x (FY22) to 7.0x (FY26).

## Bear case and our response
Nine of the eleven single bear changes flip the rating to SELL.

1. **Flow-through: history says most savings are reinvested.** 40% gives 1,505p (SELL by 0.8p); 33% gives 1,482p; 25% gives 1,456p. **Response:** 46% applies the same low-end-of-guidance rule as sales growth; PM to respond on whether the track record (FY23: +15bps margin on GBP 450m of savings) warrants a lower base.
2. **Terminal returns: ROIC fell 5 points in three years.** RONIC 10.8% (capex 7.07%) gives 1,460p (SELL); RONIC equal to WACC gives 1,368p. **Response:** 13.4% is the latest and lowest ROIC in four years, not a recovery assumption; PM to respond if returns keep falling.
3. **No recovery: management missed its 2023-26 targets.** Zero organic growth gives 1,390p (-135p, SELL). **Response:** PM to respond; the growth path is the low end of guidance, which management has not met recently.
4. **Terminal growth 2% vs falling spirits volumes.** 1.5% gives 1,470p (-55p, SELL). **Response:** PM to respond; the IWSR evidence is secondhand.
5. **Cost of capital: leverage after the dividend cut.** A 2.0% credit spread gives 1,456p (-69p); beta 1.00 gives 1,481p (-44p); both SELL. **Response:** 0.96 already sits above every precise own-regression estimate; PM to respond on the credit spread.

Against the bear: beta 0.72 gives 1,832p (+307p), still HOLD; restructuring tax relief adds 7p. Nothing reaches BUY (1,924p).
