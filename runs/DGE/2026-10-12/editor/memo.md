## Thesis
- **The price already assumes the recovery is delivered.** The DCF gives 1,627p (USD 21.54), 2.7% below the share price; the market is pricing a WACC of about 8.3% against our 8.46%. There is no margin of safety at the low end of guidance.
- **Self-help is the whole profit story.** Organic net sales fell 2.0% in FY26 and North America fell 8.4%. The c.USD 1,000m savings plan drives profit growth; we assume 46% of it reaches profit, the low end of FY27-29 guidance.
- **The balance sheet limits the upside.** Leverage is 3.14x against a 2.5-3.0x target and the dividend has been cut. Disposal proceeds (USD 3,318m gross) had not been received at 30 June 2026.

## Fundamentals
- **Growth has stalled:** organic growth was 6.5% (FY23), -0.6%, 1.7% and -2.0% (FY26). Africa (13.3%) and Latin America & Caribbean (7.7%) grew.
- **Margins compressed:** operating margin before exceptionals fell from 31.1% (FY22) to 28.9% (FY26).
- **Cash improved:** free cash flow rose from USD 2,235m (FY23) to USD 3,211m (FY26), helped by lower capex (6.1% of sales in FY26).
- **Returns fell:** ROIC went from 18.4% (FY23) to 13.4% (FY26); interest cover fell from 13.6x (FY22) to 7.0x (FY26).

## Risks
- **Beta:** we use 0.96 (bottom-up), above all six regressions of Diageo's own shares (0.37 to 0.77). Beta is the largest lever: 0.72 gives 1,930p (BUY by 6p); SELL needs a beta above 1.078.
- **Volatility:** 31.6% (1 year) vs 12.5% for the FTSE 100.
- **Drawdown:** maximum -66.8%, from 4,098p (4 Jan 2022) to 1,362p (24 Mar 2026).
- **Leverage:** 3.14x adjusted net borrowings / adjusted EBITDA vs a 2.5-3.0x target; about 2.6x after the disposal proceeds, before tax on them.

## Bear case and our response
Three of the eleven single bear changes flip the rating to SELL (below 1,506p); one bull change (beta 0.72) reaches BUY.

1. **Flow-through: history says most savings are reinvested.** 33% (Diageo's 2017 "two thirds reinvested" rule) gives 1,584p and 25% gives 1,558p, both HOLD; SELL needs 9.1%. **Response:** 46% is the low end of FY27-29 profit guidance. We will check it against FY27 interim margins.
2. **Terminal returns: ROIC fell 5 points in three years.** RONIC 10.8% gives 1,562p (HOLD); RONIC equal to WACC gives 1,468p (SELL). **Response:** 13.4% is Diageo's lowest ROIC in four years, not a recovery, and FY26 capex (6.1% of sales) is already close to the 6.21% the rule implies.
3. **No recovery: management missed both of its 2023-26 incentive targets** (organic sales and operating profit growth). Zero organic growth gives 1,491p (SELL). **Response:** the path is already the low end of guidance (0% in FY27); zero growth for five years means North America never recovers, which is our bear scenario, not the base.
4. **Terminal growth 2% vs falling spirits volumes.** 1.5% gives 1,572p (HOLD). **Response:** with 2.35% breakeven inflation, 2% nominal growth already means a small real decline; the IWSR figures are secondhand.
5. **Cost of capital.** A 2.0% credit spread gives 1,559p and beta 1.00 gives 1,585p, both HOLD. **Response:** our 1% spread is already about the BBB average (1.02%, vs 0.69% for A-rated bonds) for an A-band company.
6. **USL minority at market.** USL's outside stake at market (USD 4,450m) plus other minorities at book (USD 1,328m) gives 1,480p (SELL). **Response:** an upper bound: it prices USL's minority at market but Diageo's own 57.21% at the DCF multiple, and the USD 1,328m still includes EABL's 35%.

**PM view:** HOLD. The value is close to the price, the downside cases that flip the rating are structural (no recovery, returns falling to the cost of capital) and the only route to BUY is a low beta with weak statistical support (R-squared 0.15). Restructuring tax relief would add 7p.
