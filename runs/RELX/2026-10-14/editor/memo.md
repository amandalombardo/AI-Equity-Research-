## Thesis
- **SELL on the desk's discount rate, not a firm call.** The DCF gives 1,621p, 41.5% below the price, at a WACC of 9.60%. That is above the desk's own 6-9% range (FLAG): the run-date gilt of 5.45% and a bottom-up beta of 0.9729 give a cost of equity of 10.31%. On these cash flows the price implies a WACC of 6.95%.
- **The discount rate alone can flip it.** HOLD needs a WACC of 7.41%, a beta of 0.449 or a gilt of 3.11%. The comps median is 2,990p (HOLD territory; range 1,955p to 4,111p), and the DCF's implied exit multiple of 7.9x EBITDA is below every peer (11.1x to 20.7x; RELX trades at 14.6x) (FLAG). RELX's own 5-year monthly beta (0.38) gives 2,673p, HOLD, but its R-squared is 0.04.
- **The cash flows cannot flip it.** HOLD would need a 48.4% margin, 15.2% a year growth in FY27-30 or 6.08% terminal growth. Every sourced bear line stays SELL. AI is now a named principal risk (shares -14.4% on 3 Feb 2026), but Legal growth is accelerating (+10% in H1 2026).
