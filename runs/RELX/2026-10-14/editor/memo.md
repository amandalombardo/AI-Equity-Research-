## Thesis
- **SELL on the desk's discount rate, not a firm call.** The DCF gives 1,821p, 34.3% below the price, at a WACC of 8.92%, now inside the desk's 6-9% range but only 0.08 pt below the top. Cost of equity is 9.54% (gilt 5.45%, beta 0.9729, ERP 4.2%). On these cash flows the price implies a WACC of 6.93%.
- **The discount rate alone can flip it.** HOLD needs a WACC of 7.38%, a beta of 0.535 or a gilt of 3.80%. The comps median is 2,990p (HOLD territory; range 1,955p to 4,111p); the DCF is 39.1% below it, and its implied exit multiple of 8.6x EBITDA is below every peer (11.1x to 20.7x; RELX trades at 14.6x) (FLAG). RELX's own 5-year monthly beta (0.3783) gives 2,844p, HOLD (+2.6%), but its R-squared is 0.04.
- **The cash flows cannot flip it.** HOLD would need a 44.5% margin, 12.4% a year growth in FY27-30 or 4.89% terminal growth. Every sourced bear and audit line stays SELL (ERP 4.2% to 5.5%: 1,821p to 1,514p). AI is a named principal risk (shares -14.4% on 3 Feb 2026), but Legal growth is accelerating (+10% in H1 2026).
