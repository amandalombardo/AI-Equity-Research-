# RELX fundamentals FY21-FY25 (GBP; from ratios.csv, segments.csv)

**Growth.** Organic (RELX "underlying", as published): +7%, +9%, +8%, +7%, +7%; H1 2026 +7%. Reported growth in pounds: +1.9%, +18.1%, +7.1%, +3.0%, +1.7%; H1 2026 +2.7%. The gap is currency (-6 to +7 points) plus print run-off, disposals and exhibition cycling, which underlying leaves out. In FY25 print cost 1.2 points, disposals 1.0, cycling 0.5, currency 2.1. Print revenue fell 22.8% (FY25) and 16.9% (H1 2026).

**Margins.** Gross margin 64.6% to 66.3%. Adjusted operating margin (non-GAAP) rose every year: 30.5%, 31.4%, 33.1%, 33.9%, 34.8%; H1 2026 35.5%. Drivers: Exhibitions' post-Covid recovery (28.6% FY23 to 34.6% FY25) and costs growing slower than revenue in Risk, STM and Legal. Without print: 32.4%, 33.4%, 34.3%, then 35.0% (H1 2026). Print earns 42-50%. Reported margins (26.0% to 31.6%) sit 3.3-4.5 points lower, mainly acquired-intangible amortisation; the gap is narrowing.

**Returns.** RELX's ROIC, which counts all goodwill at cost: 11.9% to 15.4%. On book capital employed: 18.6% to 24.8%.

**Cash.** Free cash flow / reported net profit: 110-121% (reported profit carries non-cash amortisation); against adjusted profit 92-100%. RELX's adjusted cash conversion: 97-101%.

**Debt.** Net debt/EBITDA (GBP, incl. leases): 2.2x, 2.1x, 1.8x, 1.8x, 1.9x; 2.2x at June 2026 after buybacks. Adjusted interest cover fell from 16.6x to 9.6x (FY23) as rates rose, then 11.8x.

**Regions.** RELX reports three: North America 58% of FY25 revenue (+1.8% in GBP), Europe -1.2%, Rest of world +4.2%. No regional organic growth is published.

## Flags
- Bear margin 33.1% is FY23 including print; without print FY23 was 32.4%.
- FY22 +18.1% includes +7 points of currency.
- FY21 margin is 31.0% without a £35m one-off property charge.
- RELX publishes 2.0x leverage for FY25 (USD, pensions); ours is 1.9x.
