# Unilever fundamentals FY21-FY25 (EUR; from ratios.csv, segments.csv)

Bases: FY21-22 include Ice Cream (as first reported); FY23-25 are continuing operations without Ice Cream.

**Growth.** Organic (USG, as published): 4.5%, 9.0%, 7.0%, 4.2%, 3.5%. FY22-23 growth came from price during the inflation years. Reported euro growth was much weaker: +3.4%, +14.5%, -0.8%, +1.5% (ex Ice Cream), -3.8%. The gap is currency and disposals. In FY25, Latin America turnover fell 12.6% in euros on 0.5% USG.

**Margins.** Gross margin fell from 42.3% to 40.2% (FY22) on input-cost inflation, then rebuilt to 43.5%, 46.7% and 46.9%. Underlying operating margin followed: 18.4%, 16.1%, 17.6%, 19.4%, 20.0%. Reported margins were 16.6%, 17.9%, 17.4%, 16.8% and 17.9%, lower mainly because of restructuring. Restructuring cost 0.8-1.4% of turnover every year, so it recurs.

**Returns.** Underlying ROIC (Unilever's definition): 17.2%, 16.0%, 16.2%, then 19.1% and 19.0% without Ice Cream. FY25 after restructuring, taxed at 26%: 17.8% (the terminal RONIC).

**Cash and debt.** Free cash flow / net profit: 106%, 68%, 109%, 116%, 104%. Net debt / underlying EBITDA: 1.9-2.2x. Underlying interest cover: 20-27x.

**Segments (FY25).** Personal Care (22.6% margin, 4.7% USG) and Beauty & Wellbeing (19.2%, 4.3%) lead. Home Care earns 14.9%. The Foods perimeter being sold earns 24.3%, so the remaining company earns 18.8%. USG by region: North America 5.3%, Asia Pacific Africa 4.6%, Europe 1.5%.

## Flags
- FY22: reported margin is above underlying because of the Tea sale gain (EUR 2.3bn). The same gain cuts cash conversion to 68%.
- FY24: reported margin of 16.8% reflects disposal losses and productivity programme costs.
- FY24 leverage: 1.9x as first reported, 2.1x on EBITDA without Ice Cream.
- FY25 total net profit includes a EUR 3,373m non-cash demerger gain. Continuing profit is used here.
