## Thesis
- **A HOLD by 19.1p that leans SELL.** The move from 3,855p (SELL) to 4,258.5p comes from five PM decisions of 15 Oct 2026; the ERP cut from 5.0% to 4.2% gives +379.1p of the +403.3p. The ERP, the Bund and the McCormick price each decide the rating within a few basis points or 2.4%.
- **The price already assumes a best case for the business that remains.** At 4,710.5p the market pays 14.7x EBITDA for Unilever without Foods, against a peer median of 11.9x and a DCF exit of 12.2x. The comps median (3,902p, -17.2%) says SELL; the DCF says HOLD.
- **About a third of the value is the McCormick deal at today's price, untaxed.** Foods consideration 1,308p (31%). McCormick is USD 44.64, 23% below the USD 57.84 deal reference; USD 43.55 makes it SELL. Upside risks: a failed deal (+115.0p to +150.5p), margin 18.8% (4,497.9p), own beta 0.54 (5,486.2p, BUY).

## Fundamentals
- **Weak euro growth:** USG 4.5%, 9.0%, 7.0%, 4.2%, 3.5% (FY21-25), but turnover fell 3.8% in euros in FY25; 62% of the remaining company is in emerging markets.
- **Margins at a peak:** underlying 18.4%, 16.1%, 17.6%, 19.4%, 20.0% (FY21-22 include Ice Cream, FY23-25 do not); restructuring recurs at 0.8-1.4% of turnover.
- **Returns and mix:** ROIC 19.0% (FY25), 17.8% after restructuring (ex-Foods not disclosed). Margins: Personal Care 22.6%, Beauty & Wellbeing 19.2%, Home Care 14.9%; Foods perimeter 24.3%.

## Risks
- **Beta:** 0.8844 bottom-up (Damodaran global Household Products 0.7741, relevered at D/E 0.1924). Own regressions vs the FTSE 100: 0.43, 0.54, 0.91 (R-squared 0.08-0.30). SELL at 0.892; 0.91 gives 4,191.0p; June 2026 net debt gives 0.8981.
- **Volatility and drawdown:** 22.4% (1 year) and 19.2% (5 years) vs 11.6% and 12.5% for the FTSE 100; McCormick 30.5%. Drawdown -25.5% over 5 years, 5,470.6p (24 Feb 2026) to 4,077.7p (4 Jun 2026).
- **Leverage:** net debt / underlying EBITDA 2.03x (Dec 2025) and 2.27x (Jun 2026) vs a c.2x target; rated A+/A1.
