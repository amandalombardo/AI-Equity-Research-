## Thesis
- **The price already assumes a best case for the business that remains.** At 4,710.5p the market pays 14.7x EBITDA for Unilever without Foods, against a peer median of 11.9x and a DCF exit multiple of 10.9x. The DCF (3,855p) and the comps median (3,911p) agree within 1.4%.
- **A third of the value is the McCormick deal at today's price, before tax.** The Foods consideration is 1,334p a share (35%). McCormick is at USD 44.64, 23% below the USD 57.84 deal reference, and the tax on the USD 15.7bn cash is MISSING (each EUR 1bn = 39.3p).
- **A firm SELL.** The value is 384p (8.2 points) inside SELL. The only sourced input that reaches HOLD is Unilever's own weekly beta of 0.54 (5,027p). A margin of 18.8% (4,068p) and McCormick back at its deal price (4,087p) both stay SELL. No sourced test reaches BUY.

## Valuation: a sum of the parts
In plain words: we value Unilever as if Foods were already sold. (1) A DCF of the business that remains (Home and Personal Care, without the Foods perimeter), less the outside shareholders' share; (2) plus what McCormick pays for Foods, at today's McCormick price; (3) plus Unilever's stakes and joint ventures; (4) less net debt and other claims.

- **Growth (EUR, ex-Foods):** base sales EUR 39,803m; +0.5% in FY26, then 1.6% a year FY27-30 (USG less currency drag).
- **Margin:** 17.7% flat (ex-Foods underlying 18.8% less 1.1% recurring restructuring). Savings flow-through: none (no programme modelled). Tax 26.0%; capex 3.15%, D&A 2.6% of sales.
- **WACC 7.10%:** German Bund 3.45% (run date, PM decision 11), beta 0.8844, ERP 5.0%, cost of equity 7.872%; cost of debt 4.15% (Bund + 0.70% single-A spread); debt weight 16.13%.
- **Terminal:** growth 1.6%, RONIC 17.8%. Terminal value is 75.7% of EV (FLAG, limit 75%); exit 10.9x EBITDA.

## Fundamentals
- **Weak euro growth:** USG 4.5%, 9.0%, 7.0%, 4.2%, 3.5% (FY21-25), but turnover fell 3.8% in euros in FY25 after currency. The remaining company has 62% of turnover in emerging markets.
- **Margins at a peak:** underlying 18.4%, 16.1%, 17.6%, 19.4%, 20.0%; restructuring recurs at 0.8-1.4% of turnover every year.
- **Returns solid:** underlying ROIC 19.0% (FY25); 17.8% after restructuring. The ex-Foods ROIC is not disclosed.
- **Mix:** Personal Care 22.6% margin, Beauty & Wellbeing 19.2%, Home Care 14.9%; Foods perimeter 24.3%, remaining company 18.8%.

## Risks
- **Beta:** 0.8844 bottom-up (Damodaran global Household Products 0.7741, relevered at D/E 0.1924). Unilever's own regressions vs the FTSE 100 give 0.43 (2y weekly), 0.54 (5y weekly), 0.91 (5y monthly), R-squared 0.08-0.30. HOLD needs 0.748 (the main risk to the call); other flip points: WACC 6.52%, margin 19.7%, McCormick USD 66.55.
- **Volatility:** 22.4% (1 year) and 19.2% (5 years) vs 11.6% and 12.5% for the FTSE 100. McCormick, which sets a third of the value, has 30.5%.
- **Drawdown:** -25.5% over 5 years, 5,470.6p (24 Feb 2026) to 4,077.7p (4 Jun 2026), dividend-adjusted, spanning the Foods announcement.
- **Leverage:** net debt / underlying EBITDA 2.03x (Dec 2025) and 2.27x (Jun 2026, after the buyback) vs a c.2x target; rated A+/A1. The WACC uses pre-sale leverage (not sized).
